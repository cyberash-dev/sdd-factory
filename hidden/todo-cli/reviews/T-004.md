## Verdict rationale

Every one of the nine covered IDs has a `// @covers` marker and at least one non-vacuous assertion against a `then`, `observable_sequence`, `error_taxonomy`, `constraint`, `target` or `predicate` clause; the negative case of BEH-020 and CTR-005 (bare invocation) and every named boundary class (absent store, unparsable store, open task, already completed task, removal of the highest id, removal of the only task, clean and incremental build) are exercised. No local semantic risk on a boundary path: no promise is left unhandled (`main` is fully awaited and never rejects), no handle is left open (synchronous `node:fs`, no server), the error paths carry the exit codes from `error_taxonomy` (2 for `E_USAGE_*`, 1 for `E_NO_TASK`/`E_CANNOT_READ`), writes happen before any stdout line so a failed write leaves stdout empty and the stored bytes untouched, and no nondeterminism exists (`clock` is injected; `--help` is `read_only`). `testsUnchanged: true` and `writeSetOk: true` settle mechanics, so only one advisory finding remains: decision `advisory_only`, target `none`.
